Colour

Five states, one colour each. A colour means one thing everywhere in the application or it means nothing. Semantic colour is separate from the plate greys and never decorates.

LiveRunning, streaming, waiting on a tool. The only state that animates.var(--st-live)
PassedExit 0, tests green, patch applied.var(--st-pass)
FailedNon-zero exit, panic, rejected patch. Also every diff deletion.var(--st-fail)
Waiting on youApproval pending. Informational — never a status.var(--st-wait)
IdleSettled, backgrounded, nothing to say.var(--st-idle)

Plate greys

Striation tones, then the bevel pair. The brown people remember from Doom is level texture — the bar itself is neutral.
